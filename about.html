— THE JOURNEY • TAPSI TANDON

Every Stage Has A Story. Every Journey Leaves A Mark.

A Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Awardee and Dadasaheb Phalke Award Winner, Tapsi Tandon is a celebrated global anchor, public speaker, entrepreneur, and storyteller who has captivated audiences from high-energy corporate and entertainment shows to glamorous international celebrations.

“In short — she doesn’t just enter a room; she brings the energy, owns the moment and leaves a story behind.”

With an innate flair for transforming every stage into an unforgettable experience, she brings charisma, spontaneous wit, and her signature magnetic energy wherever she goes.

As an Amazon Verified Blogger, she also explores the world through luxury travel, lifestyle, and authentic brand collaborations — partnering with leading names such as Pilgrim, Nykaa, Fabindia, and state tourism boards.

Her entrepreneurial spirit extends into real estate consulting, communications, and creative business strategy, seamlessly blending sharp commercial instincts with artistic flair.
