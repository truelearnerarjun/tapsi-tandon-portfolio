Meet Tapsi Tandon — where the spotlight meets the hustle! ✨

A Chhatrapati Shivaji Awardee & Dadasaheb Phalke Award Winner, Tapsi is a global anchor, public speaker, entrepreneur and storyteller who has hosted everything from high-energy corporate and entertainment shows to glamorous weddings across the world.

With a flair for turning every stage into an experience, she brings charisma, spontaneity and a little bit of her signature madness wherever she goes.

An Amazon Verified Blogger, she also explores the world through travel, lifestyle and honest brand storytelling.

Her entrepreneurial journey extends into real estate, branding and collaborations, blending business instinct with creativity.

In short — she doesn’t just enter a room; she brings the energy, owns the moment and leaves a story behind. ✨
